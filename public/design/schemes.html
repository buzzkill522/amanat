Know what you are owed

Money you may already be entitled to

Being Deaf costs money that hearing people never spend. These exist to offset that - and most go unclaimed simply because nobody knew. Thirteen of them, grouped by what they are for.

Rules and amounts change. Every figure here is checked before it is taught, and the official link is always given.

Start here

1 scheme

UDID card

One free card that proves you are Deaf, anywhere in India.

Free
Why
Almost every other scheme on this page asks for it first. Getting this is step one, and it is the step most families do not know about.
Who
Anyone with a disability certificate from a government medical board. For schemes, the certificate normally needs to show 40% disability or more.
Apply
Apply online at the national UDID portal, then attend the medical board appointment it gives you. swavlambancard.gov.in

School and study

4 schemes

Pre-Matric Scholarship

Money for school, up to Class 10.

Never repaid
Covers
Fees, a book grant and a maintenance allowance. Exact amounts are set each year.
Scale
About 46,000 of these are given out a year, and half are kept for girls.

Post-Matric Scholarship

Money for study after Class 10.

Never repaid
Covers
Fees, a book grant and a maintenance allowance.
Scale
About 17,000 a year, half kept for girls.

Top Class Education Scholarship

For studying at a top listed college or institute.

Up to ₹2,00,000 a year
Covers
Tuition and non-refundable fees up to ₹2,00,000 a year. Plus ₹3,000 a month if living in a hostel, or ₹1,500 a month otherwise. Plus ₹2,000 a month for disability-related help such as a reader or escort, and ₹5,000 a year for books.

Fellowship and free coaching

Research funding, and coaching for competitive exams.

Monthly
Covers
The fellowship pays a monthly amount plus a contingency grant. Free coaching pays the coaching fee, and a stipend in some cases. Amounts are set each year.

Hearing devices

2 schemes

ADIP · hearing aids

Hearing aids and other devices, free or half price, depending on income.

Free or half
Covers
Full cost or half the cost of the device, within the scheme's ceiling for that device type.

ADIP · cochlear implant

The implant and the surgery, for children who qualify.

Funded
Note
An implant is a choice, not an obligation. Many Deaf people use sign language and no device at all.
