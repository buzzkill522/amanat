How it works

Three things, then the next lesson opens

Every lesson is built the same way, so you always know what to expect.

  1. 1

    Watch

    A short video with captions, a written transcript beside it, and a panel for the Indian Sign Language clip.

  2. 2

    Answer

    Picture questions, not written tests. A wrong answer gives a hint and lets you try again.

  3. 3

    Climb

    Finish a lesson and the next one opens. Your place is kept on this device.

Know what you are owed

Money you may already be entitled to

Being Deaf costs money that hearing people never spend. These exist to offset that - and most go unclaimed simply because nobody knew.

See all the schemes

Rules and amounts change. Every figure is checked before it is taught.

  • The UDID card

    Free. The card almost every other scheme asks for first.

  • ADIP

    Hearing aids and cochlear implants, free or subsidised.

  • Scholarships

    School to postgraduate. Fees, hostel and books. Never repaid.

  • Section 80U

    ₹75,000 off taxable income, or ₹1,25,000 if severe. No receipts.
