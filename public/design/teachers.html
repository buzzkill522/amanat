For grown-ups

How parents and teachers can use this site

Nothing here needs an account, a password or an email address.

In a classroom

  • Put one lesson on the projector and let the class watch the video together, captions on.
  • Read the transcript beside it for children who prefer reading to watching.
  • Do the picture questions as a group. A wrong answer gives a hint and lets them try again, so there is no penalty for guessing aloud.

Choosing a level

  • Every topic is written three times over, once at each level. The levels are sorted by difficulty, never by age.
  • Start at Level 1 if you are not sure. Moving between levels does not lose any progress.

On a shared tablet

  • Progress is kept in the browser on the device, not per child. On a shared tablet everyone shares one place in the course.
  • A teacher can open every lesson at once, so a class is not blocked by the unlock order.

Privacy

There are no accounts and no analytics, and nothing about a child is ever sent anywhere. Progress is stored in the browser on the device itself, and clearing the browser data clears it.

One exception, until the real lesson videos are filmed: the placeholder videos are streamed from an outside host, so opening a lesson does fetch a file from it.
