A Jataka tale

The old bowl

A poor family had an old bowl. It was black with soot. They thought it was worth nothing.

A trader came to the door. He rubbed the bowl and saw gold under the dirt. He said, “This is rubbish. I will take it for one coin.”

The family said no.

A second trader came. He saw the same gold. He said, “This bowl is gold. It is worth more than everything in my cart.” He paid a fair price and took it.

₹

The bowl was gold the whole time. Only the price changed. Price is what somebody asks for a thing. Value is what it is really worth.
